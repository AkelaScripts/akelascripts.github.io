Sushi Merge

A free one-touch sushi merge puzzle for Android, by PixelWolf.

Feed the never-satisfied panda customer! Drop sushi onto your serving tray, merge matching pieces into bigger, fancier sushi and chain merges into huge combos. Use the Joker, Magnet and Bomb power-ups, finish the daily task to keep your streak, and climb the world leaderboard with Google Play Games.
